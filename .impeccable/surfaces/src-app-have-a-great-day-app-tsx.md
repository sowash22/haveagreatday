Mode: Operate
Audience: People planning time outside without wanting to interpret a full weather dashboard.
Task: Choose a place, see the three strongest windows across Morning, Noon, Evening, and Night, and scan the next seven days.
Direction: One immersive, image-backed decision card that fits the viewport on desktop and mobile.
Memorable moment: Every refresh and day selection reveals another weather-matched scene while the glass evidence sheet explains the selected time without leaving the photograph.
Constraints: Keep one selected day first, show a compact day-only seven-day ribbon, and present exactly three ranked day-part windows as read-only results. Give each window compact weather, temperature, AQI, UV, and light evidence while keeping its time visually dominant. Keep saved places inside location selection, place a compact °C/°F toggle beside it, and keep aggregate evidence plus the normalized hourly-fit chart inside one bottom disclosure. Label after-dark windows honestly. Do not add activity controls, a page header, page footer, alternatives, preferences forms, nested cards, decorative dashboard elements, or unsupported safety claims.
