Mode: Operate
Audience: People planning time outside without wanting to interpret a full weather dashboard.
Task: Choose a place, read a personal note about the three strongest windows across Morning, Noon, Evening, and Night, and scan the next seven days.
Direction: One immersive, image-backed decision card that fits the viewport on desktop and mobile.
Memorable moment: Every refresh and day selection reveals another weather-matched scene from a full-week pool while the glass evidence sheet explains the selected time without leaving the photograph.
Constraints: Pair the wordmark with one quiet, short purpose line, keep one selected day first, show a compact day-only seven-day ribbon, and present up to three ranked day-part windows as one flowing, read-only note rather than segmented cards or buttons. Emphasize each time inline and follow it with compact weather, temperature, AQI, UV, and light evidence. Inference may personalize only the opening and connective phrases; ranking and facts remain deterministic, with an immediate local fallback. Keep saved places inside location selection, place a compact location-aware °C/°F toggle beside it, preserve any user override, and keep aggregate evidence plus the normalized hourly-fit chart inside one bottom disclosure. Label after-dark windows honestly. Do not add activity controls, a page header, page footer, alternatives, preferences forms, chat bubbles, AI badges, nested cards, decorative dashboard elements, or unsupported safety claims.
