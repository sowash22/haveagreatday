Mode: Operate
Audience: People planning time outside without wanting to interpret a full weather dashboard.
Task: Choose a place, read an adaptive personal note about the whole day or its worthwhile windows, and scan the next seven days.
Direction: One immersive, image-backed decision card that fits the viewport on desktop and mobile.
Memorable moment: Every refresh and day selection reveals another weather-matched scene from a full-week pool while the glass evidence sheet explains the selected time without leaving the photograph.
Constraints: Pair the wordmark with one quiet, short purpose line, keep one selected day first, and show a compact day-only seven-day ribbon. Let the main note adapt between an all-day recommendation, one to three worthwhile ranked windows, and no recommendation when the day does not clear forecast guardrails. Emphasize selected times inline and keep all-day or no-recommendation metrics conversational. Inference chooses only among deterministic allowed modes and candidate prefixes; measurements remain app-rendered, with an immediate adaptive fallback. Keep saved places inside location selection, place a compact location-aware °C/°F toggle beside it, preserve any user override, and keep matching evidence plus the normalized hourly-fit chart inside one bottom disclosure. Label after-dark windows honestly. Do not add activity controls, a page header, page footer, alternatives, preferences forms, chat bubbles, AI badges, nested cards, decorative dashboard elements, or unsupported safety claims.
