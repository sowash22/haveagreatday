Mode: Operate
Audience: People planning time outside without wanting to interpret a full weather dashboard.
Task: Choose a place, see the three strongest windows across Morning, Noon, Evening, and Night, and scan the next seven days.
Direction: One immersive, image-backed decision card that fits the viewport on desktop and mobile.
Memorable moment: Every refresh and day selection reveals another weather-matched scene from a full-week pool while the glass evidence sheet explains the selected time without leaving the photograph.
Constraints: Keep one selected day first, show a compact day-only seven-day ribbon, and present up to three day-part windows as read-only results ranked Best, Next best, and Third best from left to right. Give each window compact weather, temperature, AQI, UV, and light evidence while keeping its time visually dominant. Keep saved places inside location selection, place a compact location-aware °C/°F toggle beside it, preserve any user override, and keep aggregate evidence plus the normalized hourly-fit chart inside one bottom disclosure. Label after-dark windows honestly. Do not add activity controls, a page header, page footer, alternatives, preferences forms, nested cards, decorative dashboard elements, or unsupported safety claims.
