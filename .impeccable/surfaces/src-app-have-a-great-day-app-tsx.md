Mode: Operate
Audience: People planning walks, hikes, rides, and time outside with children or pets without wanting to interpret a full weather dashboard.
Task: Choose a place, read an adaptive personal note about the whole day or its worthwhile windows, and scan the next seven days.
Direction: One immersive, image-backed decision card that fits the viewport on desktop and mobile.
Memorable moment: Every refresh and day selection reveals another weather-matched scene from a full-week pool while the glass evidence sheet explains the selected time without leaving the photograph.
Constraints: Make the distinction from a weather dashboard immediate: the product combines weather, air quality, UV, comfort, daylight, and a constrained inference layer into a practical answer about when to go outside. Pair the wordmark with one quiet, short purpose line, keep one selected day first, and show a compact day-only seven-day ribbon. A usable day must show two or three worthwhile daylight windows, even when the opening celebrates the whole day; a day with fewer than two defensible choices shows no recommendation. Explain briefly why other dayparts ranked lower or were omitted. Emphasize selected times inline and keep all-day or no-recommendation metrics conversational. Inference chooses only among deterministic allowed modes and candidate prefixes; measurements remain app-rendered, with an immediate adaptive fallback. Keep saved places inside location selection, place a compact location-aware °C/°F toggle beside it, preserve any user override, and place two adjacent bottom disclosures for matching evidence and the founder story. Give both disclosures the same glass-sheet interaction, a visible close control, allow only one to remain open, and close either on outside press or Escape. Do not recommend after-dark windows by default. Do not add activity controls, a page header, page footer, alternatives, preferences forms, chat bubbles, AI badges, nested cards, decorative dashboard elements, or unsupported safety claims.
